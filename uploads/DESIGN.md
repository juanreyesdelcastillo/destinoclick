## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
The brand embodies an elite wholesale travel partner tailored for visionary travel agencies, high-value corporate partners, and luxury itinerary architects. The tone strikes a balance between institutional confidence and experiential wanderlust: authoritative, seamless, discreetly opulent, and technologically fluid. Every visual cue communicates financial peace of mind, white-glove 24/7 emergency orchestration, and peerless global access.

### Design Style: Modern Glassmorphic Executive Presentation
The visual language converges on **Dark Luxe Glassmorphism** blended with high-precision executive software ergonomics. The foundational canvas relies on deep midnight oceanic tones (#070d18 to #0f172a), punctuated by warm honeyed gold (#d49a3d, #e6b063) to evoke sunlight at golden hour over Mediterranean villas and luxury destinations. 

Key characteristics include:
- Multi-layered dark glass containers with subtle inner rim luminescence (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`).
- Crisp, hyper-legible geometric typography that commands executive attention without feeling sterile.
- High-contrast photographic viewports featuring cinematic aspect ratios (16:9, 21:9) framed by tablet bezel metaphors.
- Gold metallic accents reserved specifically for primary intent, key conversion metrics, active navigation nodes, and trust verification stamps.

## Colors

### Color Strategy
The system prioritizes depth over saturation. The canvas stays anchored in deep navy and obsidian slate, allowing the warm amber gold primary hue to deliver maximum focal velocity for key interactive prompts and business milestones.

- **Primary (`#e6b063` / `#d49a3d` - Imperial Gold / Sunlit Ochre):** Represents prestige, premium hospitality, and actionable decision points. Used for primary CTAs, highlight badges, slider thumbs, critical ROI statistics, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#38bdf8` - Azure Horizon):** Evokes global navigation, sky routes, and digital connectivity. Used for subtle category tags, secondary links, and selected interactive pins on map modules.
- **Tertiary (`#34d399` - Jade Resolution):** Signals affirmative problem resolution, 24/7 support reliability, and verified status checkmarks.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#070d18` (Primary background, deep midnight).
  - `Surface Base`: `#0b1324` (Viewport, section background).
  - `Surface Elevated / Glass`: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)` with backdrop blur.
  - `Surface Rim / Border`: `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.12)` and active gold stroke `rgba(230, 176, 99, 0.35)`.
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#f8fafc` (Headings, executive values).
  - `Text Muted`: `#94a3b8` (Supporting captions, subtitles).
  - `Text Subtle`: `#64748b` (Disclaimers, step counters, inactive iconography).

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchies
- **Headline Font (`Montserrat`):** Built for bold, architectural punch. It conveys executive security and modern hospitality elegance. All display headers and key numeric ROI multipliers adopt this font.
- **Body & Label Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Offers rounded, comfortable legibility with high x-height and open counters. It ensures long business case studies, itinerary features, and complex pitch deck text remain instantly scannable on tablet and desktop screens.
- **Step Counters (`label-caps`):** Used consistently for procedural tags (e.g., `01`, `02`, `03`) to structure commercial conversations and presentations systematically.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The framework relies on a **12-column dynamic fluid-to-fixed hybrid layout** optimized for high-resolution interactive displays and pitch tablet presentations:
- **Presentation Deck View:** A 50/50 dual-pane presentation layout (left narrative pillar + right tablet viewport preview) across desktop resolutions (`≥1024px`).
- **Interactive Tablet Frame:** A persistent 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio wrapper representing the commercial sales enablement tablet, maintaining minimum internal safe zones of `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (Single column stacked; tablet mockups collapse to responsive native touch cards).
  - Tablet: `768px – 1023px` (8-column responsive grid; narrative sits above tablet viewport).
  - Desktop / Showcase: `≥ 1024px` (12-column split master grid; narrative and device mockup aligned synchronously).

## Elevation & Depth

### Elevation Philosophy: Luminous Surface Tiers
Depth is achieved via translucent dark glass, optical blur filters, and amber rim lighting rather than muddy black drop shadows:

1. **Level 0 (Deep Canvas):** Background foundation with `#070d18` and ambient dual-radial spot highlights (`rgba(230, 176, 99, 0.05)` top-right, `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.04)` bottom-left).
2. **Level 1 (Card & Tablet Chassis):** Semi-opaque midnight slate `#0f172a` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, surrounded by an ultra-thin border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Badges & Controls):** `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, subtle outer glow `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
4. **Level 3 (Focused & Primary Modals):** Accent glow using imperial gold tint: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(230, 176, 99, 0.35), 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

### Roundedness System (Level 2: Rounded)
The shape language mirrors state-of-the-art handheld hardware (tablets, smooth metallic devices) and bespoke executive stationery:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Buttons):** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`).
- **Surface Cards & Showcase Panels:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Device Frames & Hero Display Shells:** `1.5rem` to `2rem` (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) with matching interior nested radius rules ($R_{inner} = R_{outer} - Padding$).
- **Pills / Status Dots:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`) for live counters, traveler status markers, and interactive step indices.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gold Glow):** Background `#e6b063`, text `#0a101d` (Font weight: 700). Hover produces a subtle luminous spread (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(230, 176, 99, 0.45)`) and smooth background transition to `#d49a3d`.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Translucent fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover state activates an amber border tone `rgba(230, 176, 99, 0.6)`.
- **Navigation Touch Controls:** Compact square or circular dark glass buttons (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`) with centered gold or white glyphs.

### 2. Device Viewport Frame (Tablet Shell)
- Outer bezel in satin slate (`#020617`), 12px padding with continuous corner curvature.
- Screen inner edge has a 1px glass containment border with subtle inner-shadow simulating an OLED panel.
- Header bar includes battery, time, signal, and close/home navigation indicators in muted slate (`#64748b`).

### 3. Metric & ROI Growth Calculators
- Interactive numeric counters displayed in Montserrat bold with primary gold color (`#e6b063`).
- Increment/Decrement stepper controls with pill-shaped glass enclosures and tactile pressed states (`scale(0.96)`).
- Visual trend line charts rendered in monochromatic gold `#e6b063` stroke with gradient fill fading into canvas transparency.

### 4. Interactive Carousel & Destination Badges
- Cards feature 16:9 destination imagery overlaid with deep gradient masks (`linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 40%, #0b1324 100%)`).
- Floating meta chips displaying location, season, and luxury grade with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

### 5. Stepper & Emergency Resolution Pipeline
- Horizontal and vertical sequential track connected by 2px hairline rails.
- Completed and active steps highlighted with gold numeric circles, while resolved incidents feature emerald checks (`#34d399`).

### 6. Form Inputs & Lead Capture
- Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`, 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`. Text color `#f8fafc`.
- Focus state brings a sharp 1px ring in `#e6b063` and an ambient gold input wash (`rgba(230, 176, 99, 0.08)`).
- Placeholder text styled in `#475569` for clear contrast separation.
